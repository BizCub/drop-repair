# Drop to Repair

Repair your gear without an anvil - just drop a repair material on the ground next to a dropped tool or armor piece and it will absorb the material and restore durability

![preview](https://i.postimg.cc/CKtCMG5S/1.gif)

## How it works

- Drop a damaged item on the ground
- Throw its repair material nearby
- The material is consumed and durability is restored
- One material stack can be split between several items at once

![preview](https://i.postimg.cc/VLDCLqZh/2.gif)

## Features

- Vanilla repair materials work out of the box
- Custom repair rules — assign any material to any item
- Adjustable repair fraction per material
- Adjustable search radius and check interval
- Works on Fabric Forge and NeoForge

![preview](https://i.postimg.cc/QtGBn0VT/3.gif)

## Configuration

All options are available in the config screen

- Repair Fraction - how much durability each material restores
- Radius - how close items must be
- Check Interval - how often dropped items are scanned
- Repair Materials - custom item to material mappings
